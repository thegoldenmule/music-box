# The Idea

**Status:** active

## Body
A native iOS and Mac app that lets kids explore music inside walls the parent sets. It plays from Apple Music through MusicKit, so the catalog is complete; we control which parts of it a kid can reach.

## Two halves of one app

- **Parent admin**. Behind a lock. Set up each kid, pick their sandbox level, build their allow and deny lists by searching the real catalog, and read reports on what they've been doing.
- **Kid player**. Big, simple, no escape hatches. Browse and play whatever the policy permits. No links out, no ads, no "more like this" that wanders somewhere we didn't vet.

## The three sandbox levels

Set per kid, not per family.

| Level | What the kid can reach | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Allow only | Only what the parent added: specific artists, albums, playlists, or songs. No open search. | Young kids, or parents who want to hand-pick everything. |
| 2. Deny plus auto-screen | Search and browse the whole catalog. Anything marked explicit is blocked automatically, and the parent can deny specific artists or albums. | The default. |
| 3. Report only | Everything. No walls. | Older kids the parent trusts but wants visibility into. |

Reporting runs in all three levels. Level 3 is the same app with the walls taken down, not a separate mode. One policy engine, one log, three configurations.

## Reports

- Every search the kid typed and every song that played.
- What got blocked, and which rule blocked it.
- A weekly summary rather than a raw event log.
